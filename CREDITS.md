# Credits

Z Battle Yard was designed and developed by **Miguel Rodríguez Gallego** during his internship at ESNE Asturias (2022–2023).

## Original work

Everything not listed under *Third-party assets* is original work. That includes:

- All gameplay code (`Assets/Scripts`), apart from the adapted snippets listed below
- The train station level, the zombie model, the player arms, the Mystery Box, perks and power-ups
- Textures made in Substance Painter, the UI, icons and logo
- The *Main Menu* and *Battle Theme* music, and the UI, wave, power-up and Mystery Box sound effects
  (FL Studio projects in `ArtSource/Audio`)
- Particle effects built with Unity's particle system and VFX Graph, except the explosion

Editable sources (Blender, Photoshop, Illustrator, FL Studio) are in [`ArtSource/`](ArtSource).
